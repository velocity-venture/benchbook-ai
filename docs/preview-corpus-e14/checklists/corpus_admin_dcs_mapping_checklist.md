# Corpus Admin DCS Mapping Checklist

Date: 2026-06-29

## Before review

- Confirm DCS material remains guardrail/reference only.
- Confirm DCS production-eligible count remains zero.
- Confirm the mapping record remains metadata-only.
- Confirm the review does not infer authority from title, topic, or filename alone.

## Review steps

- Verify source file.
- Verify source hash.
- Verify DCS chapter.
- Verify policy number if available.
- Verify document type.
- Classify as document-anchored guardrail, map-to-policy candidate, archive candidate, or production exclusion.
- Record reviewer fields.
- Record owner escalation if production treatment is proposed.

## Stop conditions

- Source currency cannot be verified.
- Policy identity cannot be verified.
- Mapping requires legal body text in a committed artifact.
- Production-answer authority is proposed without a later approved workflow.
